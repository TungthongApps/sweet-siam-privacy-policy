Sweet Siam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sweet Siam game ("the app") handles. The app is published by Tungthong Apps ("we", "us").

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, scores, Golden Coconuts, power-ups, Dessert Dex and achievements) and your settings (such as volume, haptics and language) are saved on your device. We don't have servers, and we never receive this information. Unless you use Google Play Games (below), uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app has no accounts of its own and no chat, and it doesn't ask for your name, email or contacts.

Google Play Games (Android, optional)

On Android, you can sign in to Google Play Games, which Play Games may also do for you when the game starts if you've used it before. While you're signed in:

  • your achievements and your total stars (for the star leaderboard) are sent to Google Play Games, where you and, depending on your Play Games settings, other players can see them;
  • a copy of your game progress (levels, stars, scores, Golden Coconuts, power-ups, Dessert Dex and achievements) is saved with your Play Games account, so you can keep it on a new phone or after reinstalling. Your settings stay on your device.

The app receives your Play Games player name and player ID only to do this; they aren't sent anywhere else. Google handles this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in, and you can manage or delete your Play Games profile and game data in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch one, for example to keep playing a level or to get a free power-up. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location (from your IP address)
  • how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics such as crash logs

Google uses this information as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising in Settings → Privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device (the menu names vary by device).

Your rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

For the purposes of the GDPR and UK GDPR, Tungthong Apps is the controller of personal data processed through the app, and you can reach us at tungthongapps@gmail.com. Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it collects and for your Google Play Games account data.

Legal basis. We rely on your consent to show personalised ads and to store or read identifiers on your device for advertising. If you don't consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which it may do on the basis of legitimate interests as described in Google's policies.

Your rights. You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options (this doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We don't keep any personal data on our own servers, so most requests about advertising data are handled by Google. Email us and we'll help where we can.

Your rights in the United States

This section applies to residents of US states with consumer privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA.

Information collected. Through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the following categories of personal information may be collected: identifiers (such as your advertising ID and IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (such as ad interactions and app diagnostics), and approximate geolocation.

Sale and sharing. We don't sell personal information for money. However, making this information available to Google for targeted advertising may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or as "targeted advertising", under some state laws.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to access, correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale, sharing or use for targeted advertising. You can opt out at any time in Settings → Privacy options. For other requests, email tungthongapps@gmail.com. We won't discriminate against you for using any of these rights.

Data retention

Game progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the app. If you use Google Play Games, the cloud copy of your progress, your achievements and your leaderboard scores stay with your Play Games account until you delete them. Google keeps advertising and Play Games data according to its own retention policies.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
